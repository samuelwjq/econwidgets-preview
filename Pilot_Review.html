Economics Widgets
Stage 4 Pilot Review

Pilot files are ready for review. Browser acceptance is incomplete. No GitHub changes were made. Stage 5 has not started.

The pilot covers the Index, Demand And Supply (Single Shift), and Government Measures To Correct Positive Externalities. To use those links, open this report from the extracted ZIP folder. The remaining 15 widgets are unchanged; all 18 originals are preserved.

Changes to review

  • The common square diagram and measured labels; parallel curve labels follow the actual curves' vertical order.
  • Compact controls, topic links, lighter glossary separators, and inline expansion hints.
  • Smaller Reset Diagram and visible Copy Diagram buttons; an Export Diagram menu with Colour/Grayscale and SVG/PNG options.
  • A single-row area legend with permanent reserved space. Complete qualifiers and the narrow Joint Provision annotation can wrap without shortening their text.
  • Shared CSS/presentation resources; original calculations, teaching text, controls, defaults, tooltip callbacks and reset handlers retained.
